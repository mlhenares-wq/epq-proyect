## Method and rules applied

- **Snippet cap.** The compiler only saw search-engine excerpts. Credibility is therefore **capped at 2** unless several independent sources in this file (or primary data) give the same fact. Where only a headline was visible and the substance cannot be checked, credibility is **6**.
- **Ministerial claims.** These are rated on whether the *claim* is factually confirmed, not just whether the minister said it. Where a claim conflicts with better data (e.g. IMB figures), it gets **4**.
- **State media.** Bernama, Antara, VOV, Tuoi Tre and similar outlets are graded **D** when the story is about their own government's actions or claims (rubric: "state-controlled media on topics touching their government"). They are graded **C** when they report foreign or neutral matters with no clear government stake.
- **Government-linked but editorially professional papers** (Straits Times, Business Times, Today, The Star, NST) are graded **C**, as the rubric lists the ST and Jakarta Post under C. Ownership links are noted in the table.
- **Primary official documents.** ReCAAP ISC data counts as **A** (rubric example). MINDEF releases count as **B** (an official government statement, valid for what the government did).
- **NewspaperSG issue links** are primary sources from the time (2004–2006). However, the link opens a whole day's issue, so the exact article and headline still need checking.
- **Ownership** comes from general knowledge of the press landscape. It was not verified page by page.
