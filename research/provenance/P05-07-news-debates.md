## Method and limits

- **Files assessed:** `sources/05-news-redsea-malacca.md` (55 entries) and `sources/07-counterarguments-primary.md` (23 debate entries + 11 primary documents, P1–P11). Section C of file 07 ("leads to check") contains no sources, so it is not graded.
- **What the grades rest on:** the research agents saw every source only as a search snippet. Under the rubric note, credibility is therefore **capped at 2** unless several independent sources give the same fact or figure. A **1** below means the fact is the original document/data, or that it is corroborated independently (in the files or in the checks below).
- **Rows are graded as written in the source files.** Where an entry bundles two outlets (e.g. "Infobae / Moncloa.com"), the grade reflects the weaker link for the fact being cited.
- **Primary documents** (UN resolutions, treaties, official statements): the OPVL note asks what the issuing body wanted and whether the text is self-justifying. **Counter-argument sources** (file 07, Part 1): the OPVL note asks whether the author has an agenda.
- **Duplicates across the files** are graded in each place, consistently: 05 #46 = 07 1b (Gulf News 2006); 05 #48 and 07 1c share Business Insurance (2006); 05 #52 = 07 2b (Maritime Executive 2025); 07 1c = P10 (MINDEF 2016).
