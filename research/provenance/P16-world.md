# P16. Provenance and reliability: world press (source file 16)

Assessed 20 October 2026 against `research/provenance-rubric.md` (Admiralty Code A–F / 1–6, OPVL, usefulness 1–5).
Source file: `research/sources/16-news-world.md` (141 items).

**How these grades were set**
- The compiler of file 16 saw **every item only as a search snippet** (no full article opened). Under the rubric note, credibility is therefore **capped at 2** unless several independent items in the file give the same fact. Opinion, spin or single-source claims get **3**. Claims that conflict with better sources get **4**.
- **D** is used for state- or party-controlled media on topics touching their government (rubric section 1), even when the figure later checks out. A D source can still be useful, but only as evidence of what that government *claims* or how it frames events.
- **Press freedom context (RSF World Press Freedom Index 2026, published 30 April 2026; out of 180):** Brazil 52, France 25, South Korea 47, Japan 62, United States 64, Argentina 98 (down 11), Mexico 122, Sri Lanka 134, Hong Kong 140, India 157, Russia 172, China 178. These were checked in web search reports of the index (RSF, Nippon.com/Jiji, Barents Observer, Buenos Aires Times, CFHK). Ranks for other countries were not checked this round, so they are left out.
- Ownership is given as known from general reference. Where it is in doubt it is marked as such.
