# Data book: Suez Canal / Red Sea (1967–75 closure vs 2023–26 Houthi crisis)

**How this was built (20 October 2026).** Figures were taken from the research files in `research/sources/` (cited as F01–F16, with the item number used there) and from `research/attribution/A2-attribution-suez.md` (cited as A2). A small number come from 15 web searches made for this data book (marked **WS**). WebFetch was blocked, so **no page was opened in full**. WS figures come from search-result snippets, and many file figures also come from snippets (see the notes at the top of each source file). **Check every number on the original page before quoting it.**

**Column rules.** *Second source?* = **Y** only if a separate, independent source in the files or searches gives the same or a consistent value. Where sources disagree, the values are listed **side by side** in the same table and marked ⚠. "NO DATA FOUND" means the files and searches gave no figure. No numbers have been estimated or filled in.

## Main conflicts to explain in the EPQ

1. **Revenue:** fiscal year (−23%, FY23/24) vs calendar year (−61%, 2024). The baseline is given as $8.6bn, $9.4bn or $10.25bn depending on the source.
2. **Revenue lost in 2024:** ~$7bn (Sisi) vs ~$6bn (IMF).
3. **Attack counts:** launches (>100 by Dec 2023, Pentagon) vs ships attacked (87 by May 2024; >100 by Dec 2024; 99 by early 2026).
4. **Deaths:** cumulative 6 vs 8 (July 2025). *Eternity C* 3 vs 4 vs 9. August 2026 attack 3 vs 4.
5. **1974 clearance:** 686,000 mines vs >700,000 explosives; end date 15 vs 20 Dec 1974; trapped ships 14, 15 or 16.
6. **Bab el-Mandeb after 20 July 2026:** −24% vs −34%; daily counts range from ~4 to ~46 depending on tracker and ship types. The Africa Center's ~25/week is far below all others. Houthi figures are unverified claims.
7. **Pre-crisis premium:** "about 100× below 1%" vs ~0.5%.
8. **WCI in August 2026:** $3,159 (4 Aug) and $4,526 (20 Aug, +4% w/w) cannot both be right.
9. **Feyrer vs CEPII:** the size of the distance effect is contested.
