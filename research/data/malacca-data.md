# Data book: Strait of Malacca (and Singapore Strait) case study

**Covers:** the 1990s–2005 piracy peak, the fall after 2006, and 2025–26.
**Compiled:** 20 October 2026, from `research/sources/` files 02, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11 and 15, from `research/attribution/A3-attribution-malacca.md`, and from 15 web searches. Page fetching was blocked, so every web figure comes from search-result text only. **Open the page before you quote a figure.**

**How to read this file**
- "Second source? = Y" means at least two independent sources in the research give the same value. "N" means only one source was seen.
- **Conflicts** are listed side by side in a small table under each measure.
- **NO DATA FOUND** means none of the gathered material and none of the searches produced a figure. No number has been estimated or filled in.
- "(derived)" marks a figure calculated from other figures in this file, not reported by a source.
- **Count definitions differ.** The IMB counts "Malacca Strait", "Singapore Straits" and "Indonesia" as separate, non-overlapping areas. ReCAAP (from 2007) counts the "Straits of Malacca and Singapore" (SOMS) together. Do not mix the two series in one chart.
